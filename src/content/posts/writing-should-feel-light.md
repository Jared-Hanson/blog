title: "Writing Should Feel Light"
description: "A short note about keeping the friction low enough that writing can actually happen."
date: 2026-05-20
tags:

---

The hardest part of a personal blog is often not the writing. It is everything wrapped around the writing.

Themes, plugins, dashboards, migrations, analytics, settings, hosting decisions. Each one is reasonable on its own, but together they can make a tiny essay feel like a project.

I wanted a setup where the path from idea to published page is boring:

1. Open a Markdown file.
2. Write.
3. Push.

That is enough.

If I ever want to make the site fancier later, I can. But if the tooling asks for too much attention up front, it quietly becomes the thing I am maintaining instead of the thing that helps me think.
